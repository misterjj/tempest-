I

A whole mood in one click

Pick a mood, and the weather, the time of day and the filters change together, smoothly, for every player.

  • 12 moods ready to use: bright sun, stormy night, blizzard, toxic mist, sandstorm, forge…
  • Apply it to the whole scene or to one zone only.
  • Changed your mind? Undo brings everything back.
  • Save your own moods and find them again in every room.

II

Twelve kinds of weather

Rain, snow, hail, fog, sand, wind, embers, leaves, fireflies, clouds, sun rays and heat haze. Stack them, tune them, hide them with an eye.

  • Set the direction with an arrow, then speed, intensity, scale and colour.
  • Natural variations: gusts, turbulence, patches, different sizes.
  • Extras for each weather: lightning and splashes for rain, near flakes for snow, glow for embers…

III

Paint where it rains

Dry under the roof, wet in the street. Paint your zones like in an image editor: no shapes to draw and stack.

  • Brush, rectangle, circle, polygon and eraser, from the Tempest! tool in Owlbear's toolbar.
  • Name your zones and give each one its own weather, time of day and filters.
  • A soft edge blends the fog into the rest of the map.
  • You see the zone tinted while you paint. Players only see the weather.

IV

Night falls, torches light up

Slide from dawn to night on a sky gradient. Then light the way: lights pierce the darkness around them.

  • Dawn, day, dusk and night, each with its own colour.
  • Let time pass by itself: a whole day in 24 minutes, or in real time.
  • Right-click a token, Add a light: the torch follows it.
  • Torch, lantern, campfire, candle or magic, with a flicker you can tune.

V

Water that flows, lava that glows

Trace a river the way it runs: the current follows your stroke, around rocks and down waterfalls.

  • River, pool, waterfall, shore, lava and heavy smoke.
  • Obstacles split the current. Sources make water spring and smoke spill out.
  • Hide passes the flow under a bridge or behind a ledge.
  • At night, lava lights up its surroundings.

VI

Filters, flashes and quakes

Turn a scene into a memory, then shake the table: one-shot effects play for every player at once.

  • Sepia, black and white, vignette, grain, glitch, blur, brightness, contrast, saturation and hue.
  • Lightning flashes the whole scene, a gust sweeps it, a quake shakes the map.
  • Paint a zone to put a filter on that spot only.
  • Every change rolls in smoothly, over the transition time you choose.

VII

Realistic or cartoon

Drawn maps deserve drawn weather. The cartoon style uses flat colours, crisp edges and ink lines, for every weather and every fluid.

Questions

Before you ask

Do players need to install anything?

No. Once the GM enables the extension in the room, everyone sees the weather.

Who controls the weather?

The GM. Players can only turn effects off on their own screen and choose their quality, in the Tempest! menu.

Will it slow down my players?

Each player's computer draws the effects itself. Quality adapts on its own, and each player can lower it. Limits per scene keep zones, effects and lights in check; raise them in Settings if your table can take it.

Do lights and night change what tokens can see?

No. Tempest! is purely visual: it does not replace Owlbear Rodeo's fog of war or dynamic fog.

Where are my own moods saved?

In your browser. You find them in every room you open with it, but not on another computer.

Which languages are available?

English and French. The interface follows your browser's language.
